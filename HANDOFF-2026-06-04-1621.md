## 🧠 Context to carry forward
- **The big lesson (flushed to KB `[[lean-hermitian-subspace-partner-not-witt]]`):** three atoms that
  looked like they needed *Witt's extension theorem for Hermitian forms* (and Aristotle's `ugen` job
  literally reported "needs Witt, hundreds of lines") were NOT Witt-hard. The breach:
  - **`hPP`** (∃ isotropic `v`, `⟨x,v⟩=1, ⟨y,v⟩=0`): get `u⊥y` with `⟨x,u⟩≠0` (pure linear algebra,
    NO isotropy — pivot-coordinate functional), rescale `w₁=⟨x,u⟩⁻¹·u`, set `v=w₁+t·x` with
    `t+star t=-⟨w₁,w₁⟩`. `v∈span{x,u}⊆y⊥` for free; the x-correction is *effective* because
    `⟨x,w₁⟩=1≠0`. `-(⟨w,w⟩)` is always a trace value (`Algebra.trace_surjective` + sum-of-norms).
  - **`hT2`** (perp-transitivity): block-triviality only EVER calls it on a NON-perp pair (the
    separator `S` has `⟨y,S⟩≠0`). A two-transvection move with both centres in `x⊥` fixes `x` (since
    `τ_{u,a}·x = x` iff `⟨u,x⟩=0`). So `hT2` weakens to the non-perp case and is discharged.
- **The two REMAINING atoms are genuinely group-level Witt/Eichler:**
  - **`hgen`** — unitary transvections generate `SU` (the Witt generation theorem). Aristotle `ugen`
    (`a1c167e7`, DONE COMPLETE_WITH_ERRORS) confirmed this is the real multi-hundred-line core; it
    reproduced only transvection algebra we already have. Nothing to port.
  - **`hT1`** — `Stab[line x]` transitive on isotropic points NON-perp to `[x]`. The source `y` is
    non-perp to `x`, so its transvection centre is NOT in `x⊥` — needs the Eichler/Siegel
    transformation. **AT ARISTOTLE** (`19b0b4a0`, still `IN_PROGRESS` after ~1h; may return
    Witt-blocked like ugen). **Local route I worked out but did NOT build** (it's ~100+ lines and
    duplicates t1): normalize `⟨x,y⟩=⟨x,y'⟩=1`, `h:=y'-y∈x⊥`; the Siegel transformation `E_{x,h}`
    fixes `x` and sends `y ↦ y' - c·x`; then `τ_{x,c}` (centre `x` fixes the LINE since x isotropic,
    `τ_{x,a}·y = y+a⟨x,y⟩x`) maps `y'-cx ↦ y'`. So `g = τ_{x,c}·E_{x,h}`. The hard part is defining
    `E_{x,h}` as a matrix and proving SU-membership (the sesquilinear isometry check). The seed
    `exists_su_fixes_maps_nonorth` + `uScale`/`τ_{x,·}` are the building blocks.
- **`g•x=x` is at the LINE (projective) level**, so g may SCALE x — transvections centred at x itself
  qualify. This flexibility is what makes the `τ_{x,c}·E` decomposition work.
