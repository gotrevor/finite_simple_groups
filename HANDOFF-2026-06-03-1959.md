## ⚠️ Gotchas

- **Transient OOM**: `lake build`/`lake env lean` intermittently dies with `Cannot allocate memory`
  on an unrelated mathlib `.olean`/`.ir`. **Just retry** — happened 3× this lap, always spurious.
- **`⁅g,h⁆` (element commutator)** needs `open scoped commutatorElement` — it's a *scoped* instance
  (`Bracket G G`), else "failed to synthesize Bracket". Subgroup `⁅H,K⁆` is a normal instance.
- **`simp only [SpecialLinearGroup.coe_mul]` no-progresses** on `.val` of a product (the `↑ₘ` vs
  `.val` head mismatch) when nothing else rewrites — fold coe lemmas into the *per-entry full* `simp`
  after `ext i j; fin_cases` instead of a standalone pre-`simp only`.
- **`SpecialLinearGroup.fin_two_induction`** destructures `g` into `⟨!![a,b;c,d], hdet⟩` — use it.
- **`abbrev P1 (q : ℕ)`** over `ZMod q` MUST carry `[Fact (Nat.Prime q)]` or `DivisionRing (ZMod q)`
  won't synthesize (Projectivization needs a field).
- `set_option ... in` goes BEFORE a docstring, not between it and the theorem.
