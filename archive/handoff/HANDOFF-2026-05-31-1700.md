## ⚠️ Gotchas
- **`lake build` skips unimported modules** → false green. A new `.lean` not
  imported by a default target won't be built by a bare `lake build` OR the
  pre-commit hook. Verify new modules with `lake build <Module>` explicitly. (Bit
  me on the reverted `ThreeSubgroups.lean`.)
- **Pre-commit hook is your safety net** — it runs the full build and *blocks*
  commits that don't compile. Twice this session it correctly rejected a bad
  commit. Don't `--no-verify`. When it says "fix it," the build is genuinely red.
- **`mulEquivOfPrimeCardEq` takes `Nat.card` equalities, not `Fintype.card`** —
  my first discharge attempt failed on exactly this. It derives cyclicity itself;
  `⟨mulEquivOfPrimeCardEq rfl hG'⟩` with
  `hG' : Nat.card (Multiplicative (ZMod (Nat.card G))) = Nat.card G := by simp`.
- **mathlib HAS the three-subgroups lemma + Hall–Witt** as
  `commutator_commutator_eq_bot_of_rotate` (named for its `=⊥` form). Don't
  re-derive it; a negative grep ≠ absence.
- **Aristotle: one job at a time** (rate-limited), and `submit "<long text>"`
  crashes — use `--project-dir` with the Lean in a file. `aristotle show` is a
  TUI that hangs; poll with `list`.
